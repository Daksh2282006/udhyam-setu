## Brand & Style

The visual narrative anchors on institutional solidity, clarity, and empowerment for grassroots enterprises. It conveys the unflinching dependability of premier civic and financial infrastructure while maintaining the friction-free, optimistic clarity of cutting-edge AI software. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate Modern with Tactile Subtlety**:
- Highly ordered structures eliminate cognitive overload for first-time digital financial adopters.
- Functional dignity: avoids patronizing rural tropes; treats micro-enterprises with the same architectural rigor as Tier-1 corporate treasury applications.
- Bilingual cohesion: balanced visual weighting ensures seamless English and Devanagari script side-by-side presentation without breaking vertical cadence or alignment.
- Visual cues project state-grade security, radical transparency, and tangible business momentum.

## Colors

The color palette leverages a disciplined triadic balance calibrated strictly for accessibility compliance (WCAG AAA for text, AA for functional UI components):

- **Primary (`#0A2540`)**: Deep Institutional Navy. Used for core structural framing, high-emphasis typography, primary actions, and secure state indicators. Evokes institutional authority, sovereign banking, and unwavering stability.
- **Secondary (`#059669`)**: Growth Emerald. Used for positive capital flows, government subsidy unlocks, completed compliances, and affirmative conversational state markers.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Saffron Amber. Deployed with precision for statutory deadlines, pending document verifications, advisory callouts, and cultural warmth without visual agitation.
- **Neutral (`#475569`)**: Slate Neutral. Balances reading density, muted subtitles, borders, and functional data grids.

### Surface System
- `surface-canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (Clean architectural off-white to eliminate glare in outdoor high-brightness conditions).
- `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for elevated actionable planes).
- `surface-subtle`: `#F1F5F9` (Recessed containers, disabled tracks, and read-only financial ledgers).
- `border-soft`: `#E2E8F0` (Crisp, low-contrast structural boundaries).
- `border-strong`: `#CBD5E1` (Interactive boundaries, inputs, and toggles).

### Data Visualization & Dialectics
- Chart Green: `#10B981` (Disbursed capital, revenue lift)
- Chart Blue: `#1E40AF` (Working capital, institutional loan capacity)
- Chart Amber: `#F59E0B` (Pending claims, operational expense)
- Chart Slate: `#94A3B8` (Baseline threshold, prior period comparison)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes high-legibility geometry across multiple scripts. 

- **Primary Display & Headings (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Crisp apertures and wide counters maintain legibility on lower-dpi mobile displays under direct sunlight. Used for brand hierarchy, milestone indicators, metrics, and card titles.
- **Body & Complex Data (`Inter`)**: Neutral, tabular-friendly, high x-height typeface engineered for information density, scheme requirements, and multi-lingual pairing with Devanagari system fonts.
- **Bilingual Typographic Rules**:
  - Secondary script translations (e.g., Hindi subtitle alongside an English label) must be styled using `bilingual-indicator` in 60% opacity of the parent color, positioned on a `0.25rem` gap beneath or following the main text.
  - Numbers, monetary values (₹), and loan interest rates always render with tabular figures (`tnum`) using `numeric-data` to ensure perfect column scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a strict responsive fluid grid structured on an 8-point base spatial unit, optimized for single-hand mobile operations transitioning to wide administrative dashboards:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem` (`16px`), `margin: 1rem` (`16px`). Primary navigation collapses to an anchored persistent bottom action sheet; touch targets maintain a minimum dimension of `48px` with at least `8px` separating space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, `gutter-md: 1.5rem` (`24px`), `margin-md: 2rem` (`32px`). Split layouts activate for business profile vs. actionable compliance feeds.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, `gutter-lg: 2rem` (`32px`), `margin-lg: 3rem` (`48px`). Maximum content container cap is fixed at `1280px` centered to prevent lateral eye strain during dense document audits.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient planar stratification and low-contrast borders**, completely rejecting muddy or heavy drop shadows in favor of pristine, paper-over-slate separation:

- **Level 0 (Floor)**: Canvas background (`#F8FAFC`). No elevation, serves as the base substrate.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px` solid border (`#E2E8F0`) and an extra-diffused ambient shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Modules & Open States)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with `border: 1px solid #CBD5E1` and ambient elevation: `0px 6px 16px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06), 0px 2px 4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Advisory Overlays)**: `#FFFFFF` with `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04)`.
- **Glassmorphic Accents (AI Advisory & Verified Status)**: Translucent surface layers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and a directional hairline border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`). Used exclusively for AI insight modules, sticky scheme filters, and verification chips to signal dynamic, context-aware software intelligence.

## Shapes

The geometric personality features broad, welcoming radii on major structural modules paired with tighter, disciplined ergonomics on interior functional controls:

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Styled at `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`) to project modern approachability and remove visual severity from complex bureaucratic processes.
- **Buttons & Input Controls**: Styled with `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) geometry to maintain crisp tap-state boundaries and professional clarity.
- **Status Tags & Micro-badges**: Rendered as pills (`9999px`) to create absolute contrast against orthogonal data grids.

- Metric display card: Top border accent (`3px` solid `#059669` or `#0A2540`), large numeric display (`numeric-data` style), followed by primary label and subtle Devanagari translation tag underneath.

### Input Fields
- Container: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `0.5rem`, horizontal padding `0.875rem`.
- Active / Focus: Border `#0A2540`, box shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08)`.

### Lists & Scheme Selectors

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or `9999px` (radio). Checked state fill `#0A2540` with white vector checkmark. High-contrast edge prevents confusion on varying brightness displays.
